# Revivokémon
**REVIVOKÉMON HAS BEEN OFFICIALY RELEASED!**

YouTube isn't a good way to watch Pokémon episodes and seiyadev's pokemon-tv:
* removes the original covers and descriptions, and
* doesn't have specials nor Pokémon Kids TV content.

That's where Revivokémon comes in. I hope you like it!
# Content
Below is the list of content. Movies and Pokémon Origins will not be added as they are not available on YouTube officially.
## Done content
* Season 1 to 10 - as far as September 5th, 2026
* Pokémon Generations
* Pokémon Twilight Wings
* Bidoof's Big Stand
* Pokétoon
* Kids TV Nursery Rhymes
* Kids TV Pokémon Fun
* Kids TV Sing-a-long Songs
* Kids TV Sleepy Time (with ASMR bonuses!)
* Pokémon Evolutions
* Pokémon: Hisuian Snow
* Pokémon: Path to the Peak
